What is an IP address?

An IP address is a unique number assigned to your device on the internet — it's how websites know where to send the page you're reading. Your public IP is visible to every site you visit.

showip.ai shows your public IPv4 or IPv6 address along with your approximate location, internet provider, and connection details. Nothing is stored or logged — the lookup happens live, every time you load the page.
